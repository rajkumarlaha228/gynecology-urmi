A well-woman checkup is time set aside to talk about your health, ask questions, and consider preventive care. Whether you are due for a routine visit or have a concern about periods, contraception, sexual health, or a change in your body, Dr. Urmi Sanyal aims to make the conversation clear, respectful, and centered on you.

What to expect can depend on your age, health history, symptoms, and preferences. Your visit may include a review of your general and reproductive health, discussion of recommended screenings, and time to decide together whether any examination or follow-up is appropriate.

A checkup shaped around your needs

There is no one-size-fits-all conversation about women's health. Share what has changed, what worries you, or what you would like to understand better. Together, you can discuss relevant options and a sensible plan for your next steps.

Time to discuss your concerns

Talk through menstrual changes, pelvic discomfort, sexual health, contraception, or other questions in a respectful setting.

Preventive care that fits you

Discuss which screenings and preventive steps may be appropriate for your age, history, and individual health needs.

Come as you are

You do not need to have everything figured out before your visit. Bring your questions, and Dr. Urmi Sanyal can help you understand what may be worth discussing next.

A simple path to feeling informed

A well-woman visit is a chance to take stock of your health and make space for questions. The conversation and any recommended care are guided by your needs and clinical advice.

Step 01

Before your visit

Think about your questions, symptoms, period dates, medications, and any health changes you would like to discuss.

Step 02

Talk it through

Discuss your health history, concerns, and preventive care options with Dr. Urmi Sanyal.

Step 03

Agree on next steps

Ask questions and decide together whether screening, follow-up, or another option makes sense for you.
