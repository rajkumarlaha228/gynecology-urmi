Uterine fibroids are common, non-cancerous growths that develop in or around the uterus. While some women experience no symptoms, others may have heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pain, pressure, or difficulties with conception. Dr. Urmi Sanyal, Consultant Obstetrician & Gynaecologist, offers specialised evaluation and personalised treatment guidance for women experiencing uterine fibroids.

With qualifications including MS and DNB in Obstetrics & Gynaecology, MRCOG (UK), FMAS (Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery), and specialised training in robotic surgery, Dr. Sanyal brings advanced gynaecological surgical expertise to fibroid management. Depending on the size, location and symptoms of the fibroids, treatment may include medical management, minimally invasive procedures or surgery tailored to individual needs.

Advanced fibroid treatment with personalised care

Effective uterine fibroid management begins with understanding each patient's symptoms, reproductive goals and overall health. Treatment options may include medication, laparoscopic myomectomy or other appropriate surgical approaches. When surgery is recommended, the focus is on selecting a suitable technique while considering uterine health and future pregnancy plans wherever relevant.

Advanced surgical approaches

Depending on the clinical assessment, fibroids may be treated through minimally invasive laparoscopic or robotic-assisted procedures, where appropriate, with careful attention to surgical precision and patient safety.

Care tailored to your needs

Every treatment plan considers fibroid size, location, symptoms and future pregnancy preferences, helping patients understand their options and make informed healthcare decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Uterine fibroids are non-cancerous growths that develop in the muscular wall of the uterus. They can vary in size, number and location. Some women have no symptoms, while others may experience heavy periods, pelvic discomfort, pressure or fertility-related concerns.

Common symptoms include heavy or prolonged menstrual bleeding, painful periods, pelvic pain, lower abdominal pressure, frequent urination and discomfort during intercourse. Some fibroids may also affect fertility or pregnancy, depending on their size and location.

Surgery may be considered when fibroids cause significant symptoms, persistent heavy bleeding, pressure, pain or fertility-related concerns, particularly when other treatments are unsuitable or ineffective. The decision depends on the patient's symptoms, fibroid characteristics and reproductive plans.

Yes. In selected cases, uterine fibroids can be removed through laparoscopic or robotic-assisted myomectomy. These minimally invasive approaches use specialised instruments and small incisions. Suitability depends on factors such as fibroid size, number, location and the patient's overall health.

Pregnancy may be possible after fibroid removal through myomectomy, depending on individual reproductive health and the surgical findings. The timing of attempting pregnancy should be discussed with your gynaecologist, as recovery and healing requirements vary from patient to patient.
