Understanding your care options

Specialist care, with decisions made together

These are general descriptions, not a diagnosis or treatment recommendation. Dr. Urmi Sanyal will explain options, expected benefits, and possible risks after reviewing your individual health needs.

Robotic Gynaecological Surgery

Robot-assisted surgery may be considered for selected gynecological conditions. It is performed by the surgeon using specialised instruments and a magnified view.

Approaches: Assessment of the condition and discussion of robotic, laparoscopic, open, or non-surgical options where appropriate.

Potential benefits: For suitable patients, a minimally invasive approach may mean smaller incisions. Recovery and outcomes vary by procedure and individual.

Book an appointment

Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery

Laparoscopy uses small incisions and a camera to treat selected conditions affecting the uterus, ovaries, or other pelvic structures.

Approaches: Evaluation and discussion of keyhole surgery alongside other suitable medical or surgical options.

Potential benefits: When appropriate, smaller incisions may support recovery; suitability, risks, and results depend on your condition and procedure.

Book an appointment

High-Risk Pregnancy Management

Some pregnancies need closer review because of the mother's health, pregnancy history, or a condition that develops during pregnancy.

Approaches: Individualised antenatal visits, appropriate monitoring, review of concerns, and planning for delivery and follow-up.

Patient benefit: A clear, coordinated care plan helps you understand what to expect and when to seek advice.

Book an appointment

Complex Obstetric Care

Complex obstetric care supports women with challenging conditions before, during, or after pregnancy and childbirth.

Approaches: Review of your history and current health, appropriate assessment, and an individual plan for ongoing care and birth.

Patient benefit: You receive clear explanations of care decisions and support through important next steps.

Book an appointment

Uterine Fibroid Treatment

Fibroids are growths in or around the uterus. Their effect varies; some cause heavy bleeding, pelvic pressure, or pregnancy-related concerns, while others may not need treatment.

Approaches: Depending on symptoms and findings, options may include monitoring, medical management, or surgery.

Patient benefit: Planning considers your symptoms, fibroid location, and future pregnancy preferences.

Book an appointment

Ovarian Surgery

Ovarian cysts and other ovarian conditions are assessed in context, as their type, symptoms, and clinical significance can differ.

Approaches: Assessment may lead to observation and follow-up or a discussion of surgery, including minimally invasive options when suitable.

Patient benefit: You can understand the reason for each option and discuss your reproductive health goals before decisions are made.

Book an appointment

Endometriosis Management

Endometriosis can cause pelvic pain, painful periods, or fertility concerns. Symptoms and their impact vary from person to person.

Approaches: Assessment and discussion of symptom management, medical options, or surgery for selected patients.

Patient benefit: Care can be shaped around symptom relief, health goals, and fertility preferences, with outcomes varying by individual.

Book an appointment

Infertility-Related Care

Difficulty conceiving can have different contributing factors. A consultation offers space to discuss your history and concerns without assumptions.

Approaches: Individual assessment and investigations when indicated, with discussion of findings, possible next steps, and referral where appropriate.

Patient benefit: You can receive clear, supportive guidance about the evaluation process and available options for your circumstances.
