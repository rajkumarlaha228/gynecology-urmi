Endometriosis is a gynaecological condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus, potentially causing pelvic pain, painful periods and fertility-related concerns. Dr. Urmi Sanyal, Consultant Obstetrician & Gynaecologist, provides personalised evaluation and management for women experiencing symptoms of endometriosis, with a focus on understanding each patient's condition and individual healthcare needs.

With qualifications including MS and DNB in Obstetrics & Gynaecology, MRCOG (UK), and FMAS (Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery), Dr. Sanyal brings specialised knowledge of gynaecological care and minimally invasive surgical techniques. Management may include medical treatment, hormonal therapy, symptom management or laparoscopic surgery in selected cases, depending on the extent of the condition, symptoms and individual treatment goals.

Comprehensive endometriosis care for better well-being

Living with endometriosis can affect daily comfort, personal well-being and reproductive health. A careful assessment helps understand the symptoms, identify possible areas of concern and explore appropriate treatment options. Dr. Urmi Sanyal focuses on clear communication, evidence-based care and individualised management to help patients make informed decisions about their health.

Understanding symptoms and diagnosis

Persistent pelvic pain, painful periods, pain during intercourse and difficulty conceiving may be associated with endometriosis. Clinical evaluation and appropriate investigations help guide diagnosis and treatment planning.

Personalised treatment and surgical care

Management is tailored to individual symptoms and health goals. Depending on clinical findings, treatment may involve medication, hormonal options, pain management or advanced laparoscopic surgery when appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Endometriosis is a condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside it, commonly affecting areas around the ovaries, fallopian tubes and pelvic lining. It can cause inflammation, pain and, in some cases, fertility difficulties.

Common symptoms may include painful menstrual periods, persistent pelvic pain, pain during or after intercourse, discomfort during bowel movements or urination, and difficulty conceiving. Symptoms can vary significantly between individuals, and some women may have few or no noticeable symptoms.

Diagnosis begins with a detailed review of symptoms and medical history, along with a clinical examination when appropriate. Pelvic ultrasound or other imaging investigations may help identify certain forms of endometriosis. Further evaluation may be recommended depending on individual findings.

Treatment options may include pain-relief medication, hormonal therapy and other medical management. In selected cases, laparoscopic surgery may be considered to treat endometriosis lesions. The recommended approach depends on symptom severity, disease extent, medical history and reproductive goals.

Endometriosis can affect fertility in some women, although many women with the condition can conceive naturally. If pregnancy is a concern, an individual fertility assessment can help evaluate reproductive health and discuss suitable options based on personal circumstances.
