Appointments & checkups

A well-woman checkup is an opportunity to discuss your reproductive and general gynecological health, menstrual cycle, contraception, and preventive care. Any examination or screening will be explained before it is considered.

Bring any previous medical reports, ultrasound or test results, and a list of medicines you take. You may also note your symptoms, menstrual dates, and questions in advance.

The recommended frequency depends on your age, medical history, and individual health needs. Regular consultations can help discuss concerns and plan appropriate preventive care.

Absolutely. You can discuss menstrual changes, intimate discomfort, sexual and reproductive health, or other personal concerns in a respectful and confidential consultation.

You can use the appointment enquiry form or contact the clinic to ask about consultation availability with Dr. Urmi Sanyal.

Periods & PCOS

Consult a gynecologist if your periods become irregular, unusually heavy, very painful, or noticeably different from your usual cycle, especially if the change persists.

PCOS may be associated with irregular periods, acne, excess facial or body hair, weight changes, or difficulty conceiving. Symptoms vary, so an individual assessment is important.

PCOS can affect ovulation and may cause irregular menstrual cycles, which can sometimes make conception more difficult. A consultation can help assess your concerns and discuss suitable care.

Assessment may include discussing your cycle pattern, symptoms, and medical history. Depending on your needs, your doctor may recommend examination, blood tests, or ultrasound.

Treatment depends on the cause of irregular periods and your individual health needs. Your doctor can discuss suitable options after evaluating your symptoms.

Pregnancy & family planning

Pregnancy care may include routine consultations, monitoring your health and your baby's development, reviewing test results, and guidance throughout pregnancy. Your care plan will depend on your individual needs.

Pre-pregnancy counseling gives you an opportunity to discuss your medical history, menstrual health, current medicines, lifestyle, and any concerns before trying to conceive.

You can discuss available contraception methods, their benefits, possible side effects, and suitability for your health, lifestyle, and future family plans.

Yes. If your current method does not suit you or your plans have changed, Dr. Urmi Sanyal can help you understand alternative options and discuss an appropriate change.

You can consider fertility counseling if you have concerns about conception, irregular ovulation, reproductive health, or planning a pregnancy. The consultation helps identify possible next steps based on your circumstances.

Preventive & life-stage health

Cervical screening can help identify changes in cervical cells that may need further evaluation. The appropriate test and timing depend on your age, medical history, and clinical guidance.

Menopause may bring changes such as irregular periods, hot flashes, night sweats, sleep difficulties, vaginal dryness, or mood changes. A consultation can help you discuss symptoms and appropriate support.

Yes. Support depends on your symptoms, medical history, and preferences. Your doctor can discuss lifestyle measures and suitable medical options where appropriate.

Ultrasound or other diagnostic tests may be recommended to assess concerns such as pelvic pain, menstrual irregularities, pregnancy, or fertility. The type of test depends on your symptoms and clinical assessment.

It is best to discuss your symptoms with a healthcare professional so the appropriate scan or diagnostic test can be advised. Seek urgent medical care for severe or sudden symptoms.

Dr. Urmi Sanyal offers thoughtful, confidential gynecology care for every stage of life.
