About Dr. Urmi Sanyal

Gynecology care with compassion and clarity

Dr. Urmi Sanyal is a Consultant Gynecologist and Women's Healthcare Specialist with experience in routine and high-risk pregnancy care, complex obstetric and gynecological conditions, and minimally invasive surgery. She combines clinical assessment with clear, respectful conversations so patients can understand their options.

  • MS and DNB in Obstetrics & Gynaecology; MRCOG (UK)
  • FMAS with specialised training in robotic surgery

Qualifications & experience

A strong clinical foundation, with personalised care

Education & qualifications

  • MS and DNB in Obstetrics & Gynaecology
  • MRCOG (United Kingdom)
  • FMAS (Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery)
  • Specialised training in robotic surgery
  • Three years of MS training at Medical College & Hospital, Kolkata

Professional experience

Clinical expertise

  • Advanced laparoscopic and robotic gynecological surgery

A considered approach to care

Dr. Sanyal listens to each patient's concerns, explains relevant options and risks, and supports informed decisions. Any treatment plan is tailored to the clinical assessment, individual needs, and patient preferences.
